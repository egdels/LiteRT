# Contributing

This fork is maintained on a best-effort basis and is primarily intended to
provide a FLOSS-from-source Android AAR for F-Droid applications.

The maintainer does not plan to maintain full compatibility with every
upstream LiteRT release, model architecture, operator, delegate, or device.

Contributions are welcome when they are narrowly scoped and include:

- a minimal reproducer;
- the affected model or a redistributable equivalent;
- expected and actual outputs;
- tested build changes;
- an automated regression test where practical.

Bug reports do not imply a commitment that the maintainer will investigate
or fix the issue. Focused pull requests may be reviewed, but no response or
merge timeframe is guaranteed.

Issues affecting upstream LiteRT itself should be reported to the upstream
project.
